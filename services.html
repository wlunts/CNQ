On-The-Ground China Inspection Services

Initial Production Check (IPC)

Initial production check (IPC) inspects raw materials, components, and first-piece product at the production startup stage to identify potential issues, and avoid batch defects — a critical quality control gate before production.

  • Raw material and component quality inspection
  • Production equipment and process audit
  • First article sample confirmation
Learn More →

During Production Inspection (DPI)

During production inspection (DPI) conducts random sampling when 20-30% of production finished, promptly identifying quality issues on the factory floor and guiding corrective actions before the full batch is complete — far more effective than post-production rework.

  • AQL random sampling inspection (same as PSI)
  • Production process audit
  • Issue feedback and improvement suggestions
Learn More →

Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI)

Pre-shipment inspection (PSI) conducts final random sampling after 100% production completion and packaging readiness — the most widely used inspection service in China to verify export shipment meets the requirements and contract specifications.

  • Appearance and workmanship quality check
  • Function and safety performance testing
  • Packaging and labeling inspection
Learn More →

Full Inspection (100% Inspection)

100% Full Inspection checks every single unit — rather than a sample. Our inspectors work on-site to examine each piece, immediately segregate defective units, and ensure only conforming goods are packed and shipped. This service is ideal for high-value products, precision instruments, zero-tolerance orders, or rework verification after a failed PSI.

Container Loading Supervision (CLS)

Our container loading supervision (CLS) stations an inspector on-site during the entire loading process to verify product quantity, packaging method, loading sequence, and container condition — preventing misloading, cargo damage, and costly shipping disputes.

  • Container condition check (clean, dry, undamaged)
  • Cargo quantity and specification verification
  • Loading process supervision and documentation
  • Container sealing and seal number recording

What sampling standard do you use for product inspections? (What is AQL?)

We strictly conduct our product inspections in China based on the international AQL (Acceptable Quality Limit) standard, specifically utilizing the ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 (or ISO 2859-1) sampling plans. By default, we apply General Inspection Level II with standard sampling tables to determine the exact sample size for your lot. This ensures that the inspection results are statistically valid, mathematically sound, and fully recognized by global retail chains and compliance auditors.

How do you classify product defects during an inspection?

We follow standard international QA protocols to classify findings into three distinct levels:

  • Critical Defects: Issues that fail safety regulations or present potential hazards to the end-user (e.g., sharp metal edges, exposed wiring).
  • Major Defects: Deficiencies that reduce the usability or salability of the product (e.g., structural cracks, severe color bleeding, misaligned components).
  • Minor Defects: Workmanship imperfections that do not affect function or safety but deviate from the specifications (e.g., minor flashes on handles, loose threads).

How to inspect products in China? And the workflow?

There are three practical ways to inspect products in China:

  • Do it yourself — travel to the factory, but this costs time and money, and requires technical knowledge of your product category and AQL sampling standards.
  • Ask the factory to self-inspect — free, but not independent. The factory's QC team works for the factory, not for you, and may overlook defects to protect their own delivery schedule.
  • Hire a third-party inspection company — An independent professional inspector visits the factory on your behalf, checks the goods against your specifications using AQL sampling standards, and delivers a photo-documented report within 24 hours.

With China Quality Service, the process is simple:

  1. Just send us a simple Inspection booking form (download from here, or contact us to get it).

  3. Our technical team will create a custom checklist based on AQL (ANSI/ASQ Z1.4) standards, and assign the most suitable professional technicians to execute the inspection on-site.
  4. You receive a detailed, photo-rich technical report within 24 hours.

What is the difference between third-party inspection and factory self-inspection?

Factory self-inspection is an internal process. Its purpose is to monitor production, but it operates within the factory's own interests. A third-party inspection is independent — our inspectors work only for you, the buyer, and have no financial or personal relationship with the factory.

Third-party inspection also follows internationally recognized standards such as AQL (ANSI/ASQ Z1.4), while factory self-inspection often relies on experience rather than statistical sampling. An independent report gives you objective evidence to make a shipping decision or support a claim against the supplier if defects are found.

How do I choose between IPC, DUPRO, and PSI?

Use this decision guide:

  • Initial Production Check (IPC) — Book when starting with a new factory, using new materials, or producing a complex product. It verifies raw materials, first pieces, and line readiness before mass production.
  • During Production Inspection (DUPRO) — Book for large orders or long production runs. It checks consistency and defect rates when 20%–50% of goods are produced, allowing corrections before everything is finished.
  • Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI) — Book for every order before shipment. It is the final quality gate when production is complete and packed, providing a PASS/FAIL decision before you release payment.

These services are not mutually exclusive. Many buyers combine them for full coverage, and we can help you plan the right sequence based on your product and risk level.

How to choose a third-party inspection company in China?

Selecting the right inspection partner directly impacts your product quality and supply chain safety. When evaluating an inspection company in China, we recommend focusing on five objective criteria:

1. Full-time, in-house inspectors: must complete professional technical training and pass category-specific qualification checks before being assigned to the factory floor.

2. Integrity & Anti-Bribery Controls: Corruption ("red envelopes") remains a real challenge in factory inspections. A reputable agency must have verifiable compliance mechanisms and randomized auditor rotation.

3. Report Turnaround Time & Evidence Quality: Delays in inspection reports can hold up shipments and payments. Ensure the provider guarantees official reports within 24 hours, equipped with high-resolution, unedited photos showing clear defect details and packaging labels.

4. Pricing Transparency & Hidden Fees: First check whether the inspection cost is reasonable. Clarify what the quoted rate includes before booking. Watch out for hidden surcharges like additional travel expenses, weekend/holiday fees, or extra charges for basic testing tools that should already be included.

5. Report Clarity & Sample Reports: Always request a sample inspection report before hiring an agency. A good report should be easy for non-technical team members to read, clearly separating major/critical defects, with clear visual evidence and actionable conclusions.

Buyer Tip: You can test a new inspection provider on a small or routine order first to evaluate their report depth, auditor professionalism, and communication speed before entrusting them with high-value or critical shipments.
