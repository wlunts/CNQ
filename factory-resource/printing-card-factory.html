The Pricing Positioning

In the Chinese printing industry, matching ultra-low-cost targets requires compromises: reducing paper GSM weight, using low-grade non-eco-friendly inks, or rushing the curing and binding processes. The owner's position is that they do not engage in this approach, which is directly reflected in their stable pricing structure. When evaluating Chinese suppliers, a factory audit in China can reveal these critical commercial positioning differences that are not easily visible from quotation sheets alone.
