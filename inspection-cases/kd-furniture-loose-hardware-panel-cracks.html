When sourcing Knocked-Down (KD) furniture, hardware packaging is a silent killer:

  • "Other agencies passed it" is a red flag, not a badge of honor. Just because a previous inspector didn't catch a flaw doesn't mean it's right. Big-name global bureaus often rely on rigid, superficial checklist-ticking rather than practical drop-testing and assembly verification.
  • Drop testing is not optional for KD furniture. Panels have no structural support during transit 鈥?any hardware movement directly damages them. The cost of one drop test is negligible compared to one batch of customer returns.

For buyers sourcing KD furniture that must survive international shipping, packaging verification is as critical as the product quality inspection itself. A perfect piece of furniture that arrives cracked is a failed product 鈥?and a direct threat to your retail brand.
