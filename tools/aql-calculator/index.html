What is AQL?

AQL (Acceptable Quality Limit) is the core metric of the ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 and ISO 2859-1 acceptance sampling systems. It represents the worst tolerable process average that will still allow a lot to be accepted. For example, AQL 2.5 means that when a supplier's actual defect rate is 2.5% or lower, the sampling plan will accept the lot with high probability.

Normal vs Tightened/Reduced

This calculator uses single normal inspection. Switch to tightened inspection when lot quality is unstable (stricter Ac/Re) or reduced inspection when quality is consistently good (smaller sample size).

Major vs Minor Defects

Major defects affect function, safety or regulatory compliance; Minor defects are cosmetic or usability issues that do not affect function. Major AQL is usually lower than Minor AQL.
