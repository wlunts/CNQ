What Is Inspection Man-Day Estimation?

A man-day is the core billing unit for third-party inspection company. 1 man-day = 1 professional inspector working on-site for 1 day (typically 8 hours). The number of man-days required depends on three key factors: product category (which determines daily inspection throughput, number of item types (more models mean more setup time), and AQL sample size (more units to inspect means more time).

How to Use This Estimator

  • Select the Product Category — each category has a different inspection pace, from 315 pcs/day for general hardgoods to 50 pcs/day for complex electrical.
  • Enter the Number of Item Types — multiple SKUs require switching between products, which increases total inspection time.
  • Enter the Total Lot Size — the total order quantity for this inspection.
  • Choose the Inspection Level — General II is the default for most consumer goods. Special levels are available for very small samples. Learn how AQL levels work.

Why Estimate Man-Days Upfront?

Many importers first encounter the man-day concept when they receive a quote from third-party inspection company. Estimating upfront helps you: (1) budget QC costs accurately at the planning stage; (2) understand inspection time requirements when negotiating with suppliers; (3) avoid under-inspection due to underestimated workload. CNQ's estimates are based on 17 years of actual factory-floor inspection data — not theoretical values, but real productivity benchmarks.
